Evidence base and its limits

Two origins, cited separately throughout. [INT] — a scripted interview with two respondents, Валя (graphic designer, 6 years) and Даня (mid UI/UX designer, small outsourcing studio); every quote is verbatim, in the original Ukrainian. [RES] — the phase-2a market study.

Three limits, stated before anything is built on them.

1 · n = 2. Two respondents, both in the Ukrainian market, one graphic and one UI/UX. No in-house product designer, no lead, nobody outside this market.

2 · The interview never asks about supply. Its stated goal is to understand how people search, save and use references. Nobody was asked whether they would publish their own work with a breakdown. The project's main risk is therefore still carried entirely by [?], and Persona 3 is a hypothesis with no interview behind it.

3 · A deliberate deviation. The template asks for a quote from a review or forum post found during research. These quotes come from a primary interview instead — stronger evidence, not weaker.

Merged personas

None. Валя and Даня were tested for merging and are not one persona: different jobs — she assembles a concept out of fragments, he retrieves a solution to a named component; different pains — he cannot re-find his saves and says so with feeling, she reports that the same pain does not exist for her. They share exactly one job, client alignment, which is why that job is the most strongly evidenced in the whole set.

Contradiction with the brief — resolved 2026-08-07

What it was. The brief read "Mobile: browsing and saving to a collection, nothing else." The interview said the opposite: «Eagle крутий, але він десктопний, а я часто шукаю з телефону в дорозі» — the primary persona rejected a tool for not letting him search on a phone. Searching, not browsing.

Resolution. Routed to the designer under constitution rule 12; they chose to update the spec, narrowly. The phone now gets search, opening a work, reading its breakdown, and saving. Authoring and heavy side-by-side facet comparison stay desktop.

Still open: whether the phone needs the full facet filter is [?], carried to phase 8 — one respondent, one sentence, and it does not distinguish finding from filtering.

What we do not know about people

  1. Would either respondent publish their own work with a breakdown, and for what? Never asked. The project's main risk and the largest gap in the file.
  2. How much time is an author willing to spend on one breakdown before abandoning it?
  3. Does a designer trust a breakdown written by another designer, or discount it as flattering? Bears directly on whether the "why" is read or skipped.
  4. Does Persona 1 need the full facet filter on mobile, or only finding and reading? The narrower half is now settled; filtering belongs to phase 8.
  5. Would a visible completeness state attract or deter an author?
  6. Does Persona 2 care about construction values at all? She never mentions colour, type or spacing — but she was never asked.
  7. Does the "argue to the lead in 10 seconds" job generalise beyond one respondent?
  8. Is client alignment a job this product should serve at all? It is the best-evidenced job in the interview and it is absent from the brief entirely.

Jobs — when / I want / so that

Every job names its persona and the data it grew out of. If a feature name appears inside "I want", it is not a job — it is a feature. A job backed by nothing lives in Hypotheses, not in the tables.

Main job — exactly one

When I have a specific component to design and no confidence that my own guess at how to build it is right, I want to see how people who already solved that same thing made their decisions and why they made them, so that I can build my own version from reasons instead of from eyeballing a picture.

Persona 1 · [INT · Даня · B1 Q3, B4 Q1, B4 Q3]

Gate refinement, 2026-08-07 — read this before designing anything for intent. Intent is a value and a job — the reader comes away understanding why — and not a mandatory field. It is carried through the material of the work itself, and it is easy, optional and progressive. No compulsory "justify your choice" form exists anywhere in this product. What form intent takes and how much weight it carries is [?], owned by phase 3.

MVP core, cuts, and the one uncovered job

Signed off at the human gate on 2026-08-07. Read off the matrix: importance 3 for the primary persona and not closed by the market.

The three core jobs

  1. Main — build from reasons rather than by eye. P1 = 3; the market closes half of it — Refero Styles already generates the values, nobody carries the intent. So what enters the core is not "a breakdown" but the intent inside it, bounded by the gate: a value and a job, not a mandatory field, easy / optional / progressive, no compulsory "justify it" form. Form and weight: [?] for phase 3.
  2. R2 — know it is real before opening it. P1 = 3; market = no. This is success criterion 2 of the brief, stated as a job.
  3. R3 — return to what I already found without a hunt. P1 = 3; market = partly — collections are universal, but no product makes a public pool searchable by author-declared properties on a phone.

Not R1, despite scoring 3: Mobbin and Refero already close it. It stays the entry surface (H7, now confirmed), but it is not what this product has to invent. Not R5 / S1: the same feature closes them as closes Main. Not E1: a consequence of Main + R1, not a separate thing to build.
