Questionable — entities with no job behind them

Quality mark. [RES] H5 asked in as many words: "Test in phase 3: the quality state and who may set it exist as objects in the IA." They do not — H5 is UNCONFIRMED in this version, recorded rather than dropped. No job demands it; it arrived from the benchmark, not from a person's stated need. What carries the quality signal instead: completeness on intent, plus the confirmed / unconfirmed state of the extracted values. No separate curation object is added.

Tag — closes no job; R1 is closed by the Task taxonomy and tags were cut as a retrieval mechanism. Survives as a field on Collection, never an object with a screen. Like and Follow — cut at the MVP gate, listed so their absence reads as a decision. Comment — never asked, no job, not proposed.

The whole Publish cluster rests on hypothesis jobs. HJ1 and HJ2 are unevidenced, and the supply side is the project's main risk. These screens exist because the brief's success criterion 3 demands them, not because a person was observed needing them. That is the weakest joint in this structure and it is load-bearing — if HJ1 fails, this cluster is the first thing that changes.

Navigation and the tap budget

Three global items, not five. Every candidate fourth was either a mode of an existing screen or a cut feature. An empty slot is cheaper than one filled "because everyone has it".

ItemJob clusterWhy it earns a global slot
SearchMain · R1 · E1 · E3 The brief fixes it: "the main screen is search and filters, not a feed". It is the only entrance to the main job, and the market demotes search behind a feed — this is the deliberate inversion
CollectionsR3 · E2 · R4 R3 is MVP core job 3. The primary persona's loudest pain is losing his own saves — «90% моїх збережень це мертвий груз» — and a cluster he cannot reach in one tap reproduces it
PublishHJ1 · HJ2 · criterion 3 The weakest of the three: it opens hypothesis jobs only. It holds a slot because a pool with no way in is not a product, not because anyone was observed reaching for it

Rejected, so the absences read as decisions: Author / profile — no job. Following feed — follow was cut. Tasks — the index lives inside Search; promoting it splits one entrance into two. Notifications — no job, no finding.

Flows

Four flows: the main job plus three related. Rendered by Mermaid on the dark theme and inlined, so this page needs no script and no network.

green box = a screen from the sitemap — only seven exist amber box = a state: loading, empty, error, unconfirmed red box = a dead end or a failure branch diamond = a decision, with labelled branches rounded ends = an entry or an ending

Shape and colour carry the same distinction, so neither has to be read alone: only the seven sitemap screens are destinations. Everything else is something a person does, a condition a screen is in, or a place the flow stops.

Flow 1 — Main: build from reasons rather than by eye

Both former dead ends now return: a task outside the taxonomy falls back to a property query, and a work with no intent returns to Results. States: loading, error, two distinct empties, and unconfirmed.

Flow 2 — R3: get back to what I already found

Searching inside saved works by a declared field is the thing no competitor does. The empty asks the honest question — was it ever saved? — because “wrong value remembered” and “never saved” need different help.

Flow 3 — R4: one shared picture with the client

The shared view is a mode of Collection, so this flow adds no screen — the whole reason R4 could be served without new scope. One dead end survives on purpose: no screen can make a person reply.

Flow 4 — HJ1: publish a work and confirm its breakdown

Two successes, deliberately: published with intent absent and with intent present. The first is a real success, because the brief makes publishing with a minimum legitimate. This flow serves hypotheses only.

Two dead ends survive on purpose, both in states the product cannot fix from inside: a client who never replies, and an author who abandons a failed extraction rather than typing. The second is exactly what the brief's criterion 3 measures — removing it would remove the measurement.

The target of "no empty row and no empty column" is not met, deliberately. Closing the Publish columns would mean inventing an evidenced job that does not exist; closing S3 would mean building for the wrong persona. Both are recorded here rather than papered over.

Two ticks worth defending, because they look generous

  • R3 → Work. Returning to something means arriving at the work, not at a list. The job is not closed until the person is looking at the thing again.
  • E3 → Search. "Stay oriented" is served by the task-named entry bounding the session — the brief's refusal of a feed is what closes this job, and Search is where that refusal lives.
